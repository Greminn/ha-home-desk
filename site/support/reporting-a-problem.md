# Reporting a Problem

Problems with Home Desk itself are tracked as issues on GitHub. A problem with your own home belongs in Home Desk, as a ticket.

1. Search the [open issues](https://github.com/Greminn/ha-home-desk/issues) first. If the problem is already there, add what you see to that issue instead of opening a new one.
2. Otherwise, [open a new issue](https://github.com/Greminn/ha-home-desk/issues/new/choose) and choose **Bug report**.
3. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. Include the Home Desk version from **Settings → About** and your Home Assistant version.
4. If the panel showed an error, include its exact words. A screenshot of the part of the panel you mean helps.

Leave out anything private. Ticket text, entity names and addresses can identify your home, so remove or replace them before you post.
