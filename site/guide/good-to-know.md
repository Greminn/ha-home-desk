# Good to Know

- Non-administrators see only the report form.
- Deleting a ticket is for administrators in Home Desk only.
- Notifications are not built yet, so their settings are hidden. Use an [automation](/guide/events).
- Label colours are limited to Home Assistant's theme colour names, not arbitrary hex values.
- Home Desk never starts an AI agent itself. **Queue for AI Agent** only marks the ticket.

For how it works underneath, who can call what and how it is tested, see [Under the Hood](/guide/under-the-hood).
