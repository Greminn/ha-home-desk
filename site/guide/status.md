# Status

There is no status menu to manage. A ticket's status moves by itself as you work.

| Status | What it means | How it gets there |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | Nobody has touched it. | When it's raised. |
| **In Progress** | Someone is on it. | The first time you or an AI agent comment, or change its priority, labels or category. |
| **Resolved** | It's fixed. | You select **Resolve**. What you type is saved as the cause and fix. |
| **Closed** | It's finished and filed away. | Automatically, a few days after it's resolved. |

## Reopening

Select **Reopen** on a resolved or closed ticket to put it back in **In Progress**.

If a ticket comes back because Home Assistant reopened it (a Repair that returned) or an AI agent did, it goes back to **New**, so you notice it in your Inbox.

## Change how long before tickets close

Open **Settings** and use **Close resolved tickets**. You can pick straight away, anywhere from an hour to 30 days, or never. The default is 7 days.

## Waiting on something?

Add the **Blocked** label. It's for tickets waiting on a person, a part or a fix.
