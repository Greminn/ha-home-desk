# Your First Ticket

This takes five minutes and shows you most of what Home Desk does.

## 1. Raise a ticket

1. Open **Home Desk** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **+** at the top left.
3. Give it a short **title**, such as "Hallway sensor keeps going offline".
4. Add a few lines of detail, then save.

The ticket appears in your **Inbox**, marked **New**.

## 2. Add some detail

Open the ticket. You can:

- Set the **priority** and a **category** (what part of the home it's about).
- Add **labels**, such as Bug or Task. See [Labels and Categories](/guide/labels-categories).
- Write a **comment** at the bottom: what you've checked, what you tried.

The first time you do any of these, the ticket moves from **New** to **In Progress**, and from the Inbox to **Working**.

## 3. Resolve it

When it's fixed, type the cause and the fix in the comment box, and select **Resolve**. What you typed is saved as the resolution, so next time you can search for it.

The ticket moves to **Completed**. After a few days it closes by itself, and you can **Reopen** it whenever you like.

## 4. Look around

- Select the **clock** icon on a ticket to see everything that happened to it, with times.
- Use the **search** box at the top to find tickets by words in the title, comments or labels.
- Open **Settings** (the cog, top right) to see what you can change.
